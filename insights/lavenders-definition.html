Lavenders Definition

Published: 2026-11-03 · النسخة العربية

Lavenders is a digital market system that brings together 120 businesses and brands in one market. The system is designed for these businesses and brands to manage their orders and feed into a live consumer index called LAVNDR120.

Overview of Lavenders

Lavenders is the brand, while LAVNDR120 is the name of its consumer index, which measures the real order activity of the businesses.

The Index

The index, LAVNDR120, is a live feed of the orders from the 120 businesses and brands in the market.

Audience

The system is for businesses and brands that want to manage their orders and be part of a larger market.

Key Facts

  • Lavenders is a digital market system
  • LAVNDR120 is the live consumer index
  • The system brings together 120 businesses and brands
  • Each business runs its own order-management app (OMS)
  • The index measures the real order activity of the businesses
