What is the golden certificate for businesses in Lavenders?

Published: 2026-11-17 · النسخة العربية

The golden certificate is an optional certificate that a business in Lavenders may request from the index operating fund, with its founding value based on the business's own documented orders. To be eligible, a business must have at least 100 documented orders, with each order counting only when documented by a tax invoice from the approved ledger.

Eligibility

A business with at least 100 documented orders can request the golden certificate.

Valuation

Each order is valued at $4.275, and to mint the certificate, the business pays 36% of the total of its approved orders in cash, which is the casting value of its golden certificate.

Review and Donation

The Lavenders administration reviews the request, and if accepted, the certificate value is shown once three verification conditions are met: financial, guarantee, and documentary. A business may also donate part or all of its certificate value to another business, with both parties entering a phone number and a contact method, and details revealed to both only when the donation is accepted.

  • Eligibility requires at least 100 documented orders
  • Each order is valued at $4.275
  • The business pays 36% of the total approved orders to mint the certificate
