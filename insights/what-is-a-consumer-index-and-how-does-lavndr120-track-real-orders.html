What is a consumer index and how does LAVNDR120 track real orders?

Published: 2026-11-03

LAVNDR120 is a consumer index that tracks real orders from 120 businesses and brands in one market. Each business runs its own order-management app (OMS), and their real orders feed the live consumer index called LAVNDR120.

The index is a measure of the real order activity of the businesses, and it is not related to buying, selling, or trading of shares.

What is an index?

An index is a statistical measure that tracks the performance of a group of items, such as businesses or brands. In the case of LAVNDR120, the index tracks the real orders from 120 businesses and brands in one market.

How does LAVNDR120 track real orders?

LAVNDR120 tracks real orders through the order-management apps (OMS) used by each business. These apps provide real-time data on orders, which is then fed into the LAVNDR120 index.
