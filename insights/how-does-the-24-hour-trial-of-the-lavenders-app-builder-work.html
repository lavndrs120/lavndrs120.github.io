How does the 24-hour trial of the Lavenders app builder work?

Published: 2026-11-20 · النسخة العربية

The Lavenders app builder allows businesses to create a web store and an Android app for 24 hours as a trial. After the trial period, the publishable version of the store can be unlocked with an active OMS, Reseller, or Franchise membership.

The app builder is a tool that enables businesses to build an order store, with a web store and an Android app created automatically inside the business owner's own Google account.

What happens during the trial

During the 24-hour trial, the business can test the app builder and create a web store and Android app. The trial version of the store allows the business to try out the features and functionality of the app builder.

What gets locked after the trial

After the 24-hour trial period, the publishable version of the store gets locked, and the business needs to unlock it with an active OMS, Reseller, or Franchise membership.

Unlocking the app builder

To unlock the app builder, the business needs to have an active OMS, Reseller, or Franchise membership. This will allow the business to publish its web store and Android app, and start receiving orders from customers.

Key facts

  • The Lavenders app builder offers a 24-hour trial period
  • The trial version of the store allows businesses to test the app builder
  • The publishable version of the store gets locked after the trial period
  • An active OMS, Reseller, or Franchise membership is required to unlock the app builder
  • The app builder creates a web store and an Android app for the business
  • The store, orders, and images live in the business owner's own Google account
