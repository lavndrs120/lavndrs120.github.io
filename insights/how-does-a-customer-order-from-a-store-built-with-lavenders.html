How does a customer order from a store built with Lavenders?

Published: 2026-11-18 · النسخة العربية

A customer orders from a store built with Lavenders by opening the store, tapping an item, filling in the order form, and confirming with a one-time code sent by email that includes the bank-transfer details. The owner then accepts or rejects the order from the dashboard and the customer is emailed the decision.

The customer flow is a key part of the Lavenders system, which reveals consumer behaviour by showing what people actually order, when, and from which businesses, drawn from the real orders of 120 businesses and brands, turned into a live index (LAVNDR120) and reports for businesses and brands.

Customer Flow

The customer flow in Lavenders is designed to be simple and straightforward.

Order Management

The order management system in Lavenders allows business owners to manage their orders effectively.

Payment Processing

The payment processing in Lavenders is done through bank transfers, which are received directly by the owner.

Key Facts

  • The customer opens the store and taps an item to order
  • The customer fills in the order form and confirms with a one-time code sent by email
  • The email includes the bank-transfer details
  • The owner accepts or rejects the order from the dashboard
  • The customer is emailed the decision
  • The store, orders, and images live in the owner's own Google account
  • Lavenders only checks eligibility, issues the unlock code, and triggers the Android build
  • Payment is a bank transfer received directly by the owner; Lavenders does not hold or process customers' money
