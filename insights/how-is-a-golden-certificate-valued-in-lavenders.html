How is a golden certificate valued in Lavenders?

Published: 2026-11-21 · النسخة العربية

A golden certificate in Lavenders is valued based on the business's own documented orders, with each order valued at $4.275. The business pays 36% of the total of its approved orders in cash, which serves as the casting value of its golden certificate. The valuation process involves three verification conditions: financial, guarantee, and documentary.

Introduction to Golden Certificate

The golden certificate is an optional certificate that a business in Lavenders may request from the index operating fund. It is based on the business's own documented orders, and requesting it is optional.

Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for a golden certificate, a business must have at least 100 documented orders. An order counts only when documented by a tax invoice from the approved ledger.

Valuation Process

The valuation process for a golden certificate involves valuing each order at $4.275 and then paying 36% of the total of approved orders in cash as the casting value. The certificate value is shown once three verification conditions are met: financial, guarantee, and documentary.

Key Facts

  • Each order is valued at $4.275
  • The business pays 36% of the total of its approved orders in cash as the casting value
  • Three verification conditions: financial, guarantee, and documentary
