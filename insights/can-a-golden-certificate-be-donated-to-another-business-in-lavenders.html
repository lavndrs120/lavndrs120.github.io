Can a golden certificate be donated to another business in Lavenders?

Published: 2026-11-23 · النسخة العربية

A golden certificate in Lavenders can be donated to another business. The donation can be for part or all of the certificate's value.

The process of donating a golden certificate involves both the donor and the recipient entering a phone number and a contact method, which are only revealed to each other if the donation is accepted.

What is a Golden Certificate?

A golden certificate is an optional certificate that a business in Lavenders may request from the index operating fund, with its founding value based on the business's own documented orders.

Eligibility for a Golden Certificate

To be eligible for a golden certificate, a business must have at least 100 documented orders, with each order counting only when documented by a tax invoice from the approved ledger.

Donation Process

The donation process for a golden certificate is straightforward: the donor and the recipient enter their contact information, which is only shared if the donation is accepted.

Key Facts

  • A golden certificate can be donated to another business in Lavenders.
  • The donation can be for part or all of the certificate's value.
  • The donor and the recipient must enter a phone number and a contact method, which are only revealed to each other if the donation is accepted.
  • A business must have at least 100 documented orders to be eligible for a golden certificate.
  • Each order is valued at $4.275 for the purpose of minting a golden certificate.
