Why does a Lavenders store live in the owner's own Google account?

Published: 2026-11-22

A Lavenders store lives in the owner's own Google account to ensure data ownership and security. The store's backend is built using Apps Script, orders are stored in a Google Sheet, and images are kept in Drive, all within the owner's account.

Introduction to Lavenders Stores

Lavenders stores are built using a tool inside the Lavenders platform, which creates a web store and an Android app for a business. This tool is available to OMS, Reseller, and Franchise members, and anyone can start a 24-hour trial.

Data Ownership and Security

By hosting the store in the owner's Google account, Lavenders ensures that the business owner has full control over their data. Lavenders only checks eligibility, issues the unlock code, and triggers the Android build, but it does not hold or process customers' money. Payments are received directly by the owner through bank transfers.

How it Works

The store's customer flow is straightforward: a customer opens the store, taps an item, fills in the order form, and confirms with a one-time code sent by email that includes the bank-transfer details. The owner accepts or rejects the order from the dashboard, and the customer is emailed the decision. The owner gets a notification for each new order.

Key Facts

  • The store, orders, and images live in the owner's own Google account.
  • Lavenders does not hold or process customers' money.
  • Payments are received directly by the owner through bank transfers.
  • The Android app is downloaded directly from the store page or the official site, and it is not on Google Play.
  • The store can be added to the Lavenders index after unlocking, with a link to its store.
