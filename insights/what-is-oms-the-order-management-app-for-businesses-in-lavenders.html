What is OMS, the order-management app for businesses in Lavenders?

Published: 2026-11-03 · النسخة العربية

Lavenders' OMS (order-management system) is a web and Android app that gives each business or brand its own order system, allowing them to manage their orders efficiently. The OMS is a crucial component of the Lavenders system, as the real orders from each business feed into the live consumer index called LAVNDR120.

What is an order-management system?

An order-management system is a software application that enables businesses to manage their orders, from receipt to fulfillment. It helps businesses to streamline their order processing, reduce errors, and improve customer satisfaction. In the context of Lavenders, the OMS is designed to provide businesses with a robust and efficient way to manage their orders, while also contributing to the overall health of the LAVNDR120 index.

How does OMS connect to the index?

The OMS connects to the LAVNDR120 index by feeding real-order data into the system. This data is then used to calculate the index, which provides a snapshot of the overall market activity. By using the OMS, businesses can ensure that their order data is accurately reflected in the index, which can help to provide a more comprehensive picture of the market.

Benefits of using OMS

The OMS provides several benefits to businesses, including improved order management, increased efficiency, and enhanced customer satisfaction. Additionally, by contributing to the LAVNDR120 index, businesses can gain valuable insights into market trends and activity.
