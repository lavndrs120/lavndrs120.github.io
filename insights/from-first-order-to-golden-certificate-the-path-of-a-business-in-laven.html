From first order to golden certificate: the path of a business in Lavenders

Published: 2026-11-27 · النسخة العربية

A business in Lavenders starts by using the order-management system (OMS) to receive orders from customers. As the business accumulates documented orders, it becomes eligible to request a golden certificate, which is an optional certificate that represents the business's own documented orders.

The path of a business in Lavenders begins with the creation of an order store using the app builder tool, which generates a web store and an Android app for the business.

App Builder

The app builder is a tool inside Lavenders that builds an order store for a business, creating a web store and an Android app automatically inside the business owner's own Google account. The business can start a 24-hour trial, and publishing the store requires an active OMS, Reseller, or Franchise membership.

Documented Orders

As customers place orders through the store, the business accumulates documented orders, which are orders that are documented by a tax invoice from the approved ledger.

Golden Certificate Eligibility

Once the business has at least 100 documented orders, it becomes eligible to request a golden certificate. The certificate's founding value is based on the business's own documented orders, with each order valued at $4.275.

Review and Certificate

To mint the certificate, the business pays 36% of the total of its approved orders in cash, which is the casting value of its golden certificate. The Lavenders administration reviews the request, and if accepted, the certificate value is shown once three verification conditions are met: financial, guarantee, and documentary.

Key facts

  • The app builder creates a web store and an Android app for a business
  • A business needs at least 100 documented orders to be eligible for a golden certificate
  • Each order is valued at $4.275 for the golden certificate
  • The business pays 36% of the total of its approved orders in cash to mint the certificate
  • The golden certificate is an optional certificate that represents a business's own documented orders
