Is the Lavenders golden certificate a share or an investment?

Published: 2026-11-26 · النسخة العربية

The Lavenders golden certificate is not a share or an investment. It is an optional certificate that a business in Lavenders may request from the index operating fund, with its founding value based on the business's own documented orders.

What the golden certificate is

The golden certificate is valued based on the business's orders, with each order valued at $4.275, and the business pays 36% of the total of its approved orders in cash to mint the certificate.

Key characteristics

The golden certificate does not involve buying, selling, or trading of shares, and it is not investment advice.

Important notes

There is no promise of profit or returns, and the terms and agreements are drafts pending legal review.

  • Not investment advice
  • No promise of profit or returns
