What does the Vindex package include?

Published: 2026-11-28 · النسخة العربية

The Vindex package, also known as the Lavenders Oracle, includes an oracle forecasting report that provides insights into market behavior, including orders, trends, projections, top activities, and locations. This report is available on a monthly, half-yearly, and yearly basis.

The Vindex package is designed to provide businesses and brands with valuable information to inform their decision-making processes.

Overview of Vindex

The Vindex package is one of the several packages offered by Lavenders, a digital market system that brings together 120 businesses and brands in one market. Each business runs its own order-management app (OMS), and their real orders feed into a live consumer index called LAVNDR120.

Key Features of Vindex

The key feature of the Vindex package is the oracle forecasting report, which provides a detailed analysis of market behavior. This report is designed to help businesses and brands understand consumer behavior and make informed decisions.

Pricing and Seats

The Vindex package is priced at $1,287 per year, and there are 120 seats available. This means that only 120 businesses or brands can subscribe to the Vindex package at any given time.
