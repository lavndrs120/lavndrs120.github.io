What is the Lavenders app builder and how does a business use it?

Published: 2026-11-16 · النسخة العربية

The Lavenders app builder is a tool that creates a web store and an Android app for a business, which are hosted in the business owner's own Google account. This tool allows businesses to create their own order store, with a web store and an Android app, using Apps Script backend, Google Sheet for orders, and images in Drive.

What the App Builder Does

The app builder is a feature that enables businesses to establish an online presence and manage orders effectively. It provides a platform for businesses to showcase their products or services and receive orders from customers.

Who Can Build

The app builder is available to OMS, Reseller, and Franchise members. However, anyone can start a 24-hour trial, and publishing the store requires an active membership.

Steps to Build

To use the app builder, a business follows these steps:

1. Enter the business name and choose services or products.

2. Add up to 9 items, with each item requiring an image and a price times pack quantity of at least $4.275.

3. Enter bank-transfer payment details and an optional local-currency rate.

4. Sign in with Google once, and the system creates the backend, orders sheet, and image folder in the owner's account.

5. Share the store link and build the Android app from the same screen.

Key Facts

  • The app builder creates a web store and an Android app hosted in the business owner's Google account.
  • The store, orders, and images live in the owner's Google account.
  • Lavenders only checks eligibility, issues the unlock code, and triggers the Android build.
  • Payment is a bank transfer received directly by the owner; Lavenders does not hold or process customers' money.
  • After unlocking, the owner can send the store's sheet with the business name to the Lavenders administration to be added to the index with a link to its store.
