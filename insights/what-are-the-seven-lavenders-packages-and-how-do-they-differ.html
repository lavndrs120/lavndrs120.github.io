What are the seven Lavenders packages and how do they differ?

Published: 2026-11-09 · النسخة العربية

The seven Lavenders packages are Lava, Vindex, Magma, OMS, Reseller, Franchise, and Tycoon, each with distinct features and prices. These packages offer a range of services, from market data and forecasting reports to order-management systems and franchise opportunities.

Introduction to Packages

The packages are designed to cater to different needs and goals, with prices ranging from $1.4 per day to $1,029,600 per sector.

Package Details

Lava provides market data, including the index card, monthly archive, and most-requested products and services, for $1.4 per day or $249 per year. Vindex, also known as the Oracle, offers a forecasting report for $1,287 per year, with a limited number of seats available. Magma provides a market directory and internal market membership for $100 per year, with 500 seats available.

Order Management and Reseller Options

OMS is an order-management system for businesses and brands, priced at $429 for services and $492 for products. The Reseller package allows users to offer 8 order apps to 8 different businesses, with a price of $1,354.8 and a limited number of seats.

Franchise and Tycoon Options

The Franchise package enables users to build their own market of 120 businesses and brands, with a price of $2,709 and a limited number of franchise owners. The Tycoon package is an agency to lead a whole field or sector, priced at $51,480 per field or $1,029,600 per sector, with one seat available per field or sector.

Key facts

  • Lava: $1.4 per day or $249 per year, market data
  • Vindex (Oracle): $1,287 per year, forecasting report, 120 seats
  • Magma: $100 per year, market directory, 500 seats
  • OMS: $429 (services) or $492 (products), order-management system
  • Reseller: $1,354.8, 8 order apps, 38 seats
  • Franchise: $2,709, own market, 19 seats
  • Tycoon: $51,480 (field) or $1,029,600 (sector), lead a field or sector, one seat per field or sector
