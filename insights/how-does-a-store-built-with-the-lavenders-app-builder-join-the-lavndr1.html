How does a store built with the Lavenders app builder join the LAVNDR120 index?

Published: 2026-11-24 · النسخة العربية

A store built with the Lavenders app builder can join the LAVNDR120 index by sending the store's sheet with the business name to the Lavenders administration. If accepted, the business is added to the index with a link to its store.

Introduction to Lavenders

Lavenders reveals consumer behaviour: what people actually order, when, and from which businesses, drawn from the real orders of 120 businesses and brands, turned into a live index (LAVNDR120) and reports for businesses and brands.

The App Builder

The Lavenders app builder is a tool that builds an order store for a business: a web store and an Android app, created automatically inside the business owner's own Google account.

Joining the Index

After unlocking the store with an active OMS, Reseller or Franchise membership, the owner can send the store's sheet with the business name to the Lavenders administration; if accepted, the business is added to the index with a link to its store.

Key Facts

  • The store, orders and images live in the owner's own Google account.
  • Lavenders only checks eligibility, issues the unlock code and triggers the Android build.
  • Payment is a bank transfer received directly by the owner; Lavenders does not hold or process customers' money.
  • The core purpose of Lavenders is to reveal consumer behaviour: what people actually order, when, and from which businesses, drawn from the real orders of 120 businesses and brands, turned into a live index (LAVNDR120) and reports for businesses and brands.
