Build your own order app with Lavenders

Last updated: 15 November 2026

What it is

The core: revealing consumer behaviour. Lavenders reveals consumer behaviour: what people actually order, when, and from which businesses, drawn from the real orders of 120 businesses and brands, turned into a live index (LAVNDR120) and reports for businesses and brands.

The app builder gives a business its own order system so that real orders can exist in the first place. You describe your business; Lavenders creates the store and the Android app for you, inside your own Google account.

How it works

  1. Describe your business: name, services or products, up to 9 items. Each item needs a price and pack quantity worth at least $4.275 and an image.
  2. Add your payment details: bank name, account holder, account and IBAN, and an optional local-currency rate.
  3. Sign in with Google once: the system creates your backend, your orders sheet and your image folder in your account.
  4. Get your store link and Android app: share the link; build the Android app from the same screen.

What your customers see

A clean store page in your brand: your business name on top, your image as the background, and one button per item. A customer taps an item, enters their details and location, and confirms with a code sent to their email. You decide: accept or reject, and the customer is told by email.

Trial and unlock

Each store works for 24 hours as a trial. The publishable version is unlocked with an active OMS, Reseller or Franchise membership; the unlock code is handed to your store automatically.

Who can build

  • OMS members: one store ($429 for services, $492 for products).
  • Reseller members: 8 order apps to offer to 8 businesses.
  • Franchise members: a store for each business in their own market.

Anyone can start a 24-hour trial; publishing needs a membership. See the packages.

Joining the index

When a store is unlocked, its owner can send the orders sheet to the Lavenders administration with the business name. An accepted business is added to the index, and its store link is shown beside it.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Lavenders app builder?

A tool inside Lavenders that creates an order store for your business: a web store and an Android app. You enter your business name, choose services or products, add up to 9 items with prices and images, and enter your bank-transfer payment details. The system builds the rest automatically.

Where does my store live?

In your own Google account: the backend is an Apps Script web app, the orders go to a Google Sheet, and the images are stored in your Drive. Lavenders only checks eligibility, issues the unlock code and triggers the Android build.

How do customers order?

A customer opens your store, taps an item, fills in the order form and confirms with a one-time code sent by email. The email includes your bank-transfer details. You accept or reject the order from your dashboard, and the customer is emailed the decision. You receive a notification for each new order.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every store runs for 24 hours as a trial. After that it stays locked, and the publishable version unlocks when you hold an active OMS, Reseller or Franchise membership.

Does Lavenders process the payments?

No. Payment is a bank transfer that you receive directly; Lavenders does not hold or process your customers' money.

Can my store appear in the LAVNDR120 index?

Yes. After unlocking, you can send your store's sheet to the Lavenders administration with your business name. If accepted, your business is added to the index with a link to your store.
