Vindex: the Lavenders Oracle forecasting report

Last updated: 1 November 2026

What is Vindex?

Vindex (also called Lavenders Oracle) is a membership that gives you the Oracle forecasting report: an analysis of market behaviour and estimates of its movement. It is limited to 120 members.

What the Oracle shows

  • Total orders and index value, the overall trend and projection.
  • A daily orders chart.
  • Top activities, products and locations by orders.
  • Monthly, half-yearly and yearly reports.
  • Short-range estimates (today, tomorrow, 7 days) with an estimated range.

Important

Estimates are approximate, not a guarantee, and become more useful as weeks of regular orders accumulate. There is no buying, selling or trading of shares on the platform, and no promise of profits or returns. Agreement terms are shown before you subscribe.

Who it is for

Anyone who wants to understand consumer behaviour and market direction early. Vindex belongs to the Join Up path.
