The golden certificate for businesses in Lavenders

Last updated: 15 November 2026

What it is

The core: revealing consumer behaviour. Lavenders reveals consumer behaviour: what people actually order, when, and from which businesses, drawn from the real orders of 120 businesses and brands, turned into a live index (LAVNDR120) and reports for businesses and brands.

The golden certificate is the optional operating-fund right of a business that feeds the index with real orders. It is based on the business's own documented orders.

How it works

  1. Reach 100 documented orders through your Lavenders order app. Each order must be documented by a tax invoice from the approved ledger.
  2. Request the certificate from your business page (optional).
  3. Casting value: the business pays 36% of the total of its approved orders in cash; each order is valued at $4.275.
  4. Review and verification: the administration reviews the request, and the value is shown after the three verification conditions are met.

Donating a certificate

A business can donate part or all of its certificate value to another business. Each side enters a phone number and a contact method, and the details are revealed to both only when the donation is accepted.

What it is not

  • Not buying, selling or trading of shares: there is none on the platform.
  • Not investment advice and not a promise of profit or returns.
  • The terms and agreements are drafts pending legal review.

Frequently asked questions

What is the golden certificate?

An optional certificate that a business in Lavenders may request from the index operating fund. Its founding value is based on the documented orders of the business, with each order valued at $4.275.

Who can request it?

A business that has reached 100 documented orders. Orders count only when documented by a tax invoice from the approved ledger. Requesting a certificate is optional.

What does the business pay?

To mint the certificate, the business pays 36% of the total of its approved orders in cash. That payment is the casting value of its golden certificate.

How is a request reviewed?

The Lavenders administration reviews the request. If it is accepted, the certificate value is shown once three verification conditions are met: financial, guarantee and documentary.

Can a certificate be donated to another business?

Yes. A business may donate part or all of its certificate value to another business. Contact details are exchanged between the two sides only when the offer is accepted.

Is this investment advice or a promise of returns?

No. The information is descriptive only. There is no buying, selling or trading of shares on the platform, no promise of profit or returns, and the terms and agreements are drafts pending legal review.
