Lavenders (LAVNDR120) — frequently asked questions

Last updated: 1 November 2026

About the system

What is Lavenders?

Lavenders (Arabic: لاڤندرز) is a digital market system that brings together 120 businesses and brands in one market. Each business runs its own order-management app (OMS), and their real orders feed one live consumer index called LAVNDR120.

What is the difference between Lavenders and LAVNDR120?

Lavenders is the name of the system and the brand. LAVNDR120 is the name of its consumer index.

What does the number 120 mean?

It is the number of businesses and brands in each market.

Is Lavenders related to lavender plants?

No. The name is a brand for a digital market system and has nothing to do with lavender plants or oil.

Safety and clarity

Is Lavenders a trading platform?

No. There is no buying, selling or trading of shares inside the platform. The index measures the real order activity of the businesses.

Is this investment advice or a promise of returns?

No. The information is descriptive only. There is no promise of profit or returns, and any participation is subject to the platform's terms and agreements.

What do visitors who have not subscribed see?

Only the real index card. Other screens show demo data for visitors and real data for subscribers.

Packages

What is OMS?

An order-management system on web and Android that gives each business or brand (services or products) its own order system.

What is Lava?

The market data itself, read-only. You do not have to be one of the 120 businesses.

What is Vindex (the Oracle)?

A forecasting report on market behaviour for a limited number of members (120).

What is Magma?

A limited membership (500) to explore the market and its activities through the Magma directory and internal market.

What is a Reseller?

A membership for offering 8 order apps to 8 different businesses, with a limited number of seats (38).

What is a Franchise?

A franchise to build your own market of 120 businesses and brands, with a limited number of franchise owners (19).

What is a Tycoon?

An agency to lead a whole field (20 markets) or a whole geographic sector (400 indices), one per field or sector.
