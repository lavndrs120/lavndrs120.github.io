Magma: your door into the Lavenders market

Last updated: 1 November 2026

What is Magma?

Lavenders did not start to add one more shop to your life; it started to bring you the market: 120 businesses and brands. Magma is a membership that opens a door to that market so you can know more, see more and choose more. It is limited to 500 members.

What it does not include

Magma is for exploring the market and its activities; it does not include the full index data (Lava provides that). There is no buying, selling or trading of shares on the platform, and no promise of profits or returns. Agreement terms are shown before you subscribe.
