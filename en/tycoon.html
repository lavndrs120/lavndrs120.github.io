Tycoon: lead a whole field or geographic sector

Last updated: 1 November 2026

What is a Tycoon?

The Tycoon is a Lavenders agency that leads a whole field or a whole geographic sector of markets and sees market movement in front of him moment by moment. There is one per field or sector.

The two types

TypeScopeValue
Field Tycoon20 markets (2,400 businesses and brands), 120 in each$51,480
Sector Tycoon400 market indices (48,000 businesses and brands) in a geographic sector$1,029,600

Prices are shown as of 1 November 2026. The current price is always on the home page when you choose a package.

How it works

The Tycoon brings businesses into unified markets through one tool and a simple system, and all of his markets move inside the Lavenders system. Rights and financial terms are set in the agency agreement, which is reviewed and signed inside the platform.

Who it is for

Anyone who wants to lead a whole field or sector. Tycoon belongs to the Economic Movement path. There is no buying, selling or trading of shares on the platform, and no promise of profits or returns. Agreement terms are shown before you subscribe.
