For businesses and brands: your own order system inside a market of 120

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Who is this for?

Any business or brand, services or products, that wants its own order system and wants its performance measured and seen inside an organised market of 120 businesses and brands in the Lavenders system.

OMS

OMS (Order Management System) runs on web and Android so your business stays close to the phones of the people who use your service or product. It comes in two types: services brand and products brand. OMS details →

What a business gets inside the market

  • Its own order system; its orders feed the index.
  • A business portal to follow performance and manage the account.
  • A place in a market of 120 businesses and brands; its weight moves with its orders.
  • A performance-points screen: the business sees its rank and the performance of the other 119.
  • A “verified” badge granted by the administration after review at performance milestones set by the joining agreement.

Transparency

Members of the market see the ranking and performance points of the businesses inside the platform. A visitor who has not subscribed sees only the public index card; the other screens show sample data.

Compare all seven on the packages page. Current prices are on the home page.

How to start

  1. Open the home page and press the button to choose your path.
  2. Choose the Economic Movement path, then the package that fits.
  3. Sign in with a Google account and complete the subscription.
  4. Review the agreement and sign it inside the platform.

Try it first

Open the demo of the business page with sample data and no sign-in.

Important

There is no buying, selling or trading of shares on the platform, and no promise of profits or returns. Agreement terms are shown before you subscribe.
