Franchise: build your own market of 120 businesses and brands

Last updated: 1 November 2026

What is a Franchise?

The Lavenders franchise lets you build your own market of 120 businesses and brands. Each business has its own order app, and the 120 apps are linked into one market index. Limited to 19 franchise owners.

What Lavenders provides

  • A complete ready-to-run system.
  • Hands-on training.
  • Ongoing technical and operational support.

The franchise headquarters

  • A counter of paid seats out of 120 and a seat map.
  • A slot for each business in every paid seat, with a status: “joining” or “running in my market”.
  • Adding a business to your market under the agreement's rules.

The agreement

The franchise agreement is reviewed and signed inside the platform. There is no buying, selling or trading of shares on the platform, and no promise of profits or returns. Agreement terms are shown before you subscribe.
