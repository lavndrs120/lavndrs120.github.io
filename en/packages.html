Lavenders packages fall into two paths: Join Up to follow the market, and the Economic Movement to take part in building it.

Lava — market data (read-only)

Lava is the market data itself. The 120 businesses form the market, and you read the data without having to be one of them. Available as a one-day pass ($1.4, 24 hours) or a full year ($249, 365 days).

  • The full index card and its components.
  • The monthly archive.
  • The most-requested products and services.

Vindex — the Oracle report

Vindex (Lavenders Oracle) is a yearly membership at $1,287, limited to 120 members. It gives you the Oracle forecasting report.

  • Total orders, trend and projection.
  • Daily orders chart.
  • Top activities, products and locations.
  • Monthly, half-yearly and yearly reports.
  • Short-range estimates (today, tomorrow, 7 days) with an estimated range.

Magma — explore the market

Magma is a yearly membership at $100, limited to 500 members. It offers the Magma directory of the market's activities and the internal market (business ranking and performance points). It does not include the full index data, which Lava provides.

OMS — order management for your business

OMS (Order Management System) runs on web and Android and gives each business or brand its own order system. Orders recorded in OMS raise the business's weight in the LAVNDR120 index. Price: $429 for service brands, $492 for product brands.

Reseller — 8 apps for 8 businesses

The Reseller offers 8 order apps to 8 different businesses, using a ready-to-run system provided by Lavenders. One app is valued at $169.35 and the 8-app bundle at $1,354.8. Limited to 38 seats.

Franchise — your own market

A Franchise lets you build your own market of 120 businesses and brands, each with its own order app, linked into one market index. The system is valued at $2,709 ($22.58 per business, 120 seats). You start with a $90 reservation and complete the payments as your market grows. Limited to 19 franchise owners.

Tycoon — lead a field or a sector

The Tycoon agency leads a whole field (20 markets, 2,400 businesses and brands, $51,480) or a whole geographic sector (400 market indices, 48,000 businesses and brands, $1,029,600). One Tycoon per field or sector.

Important

There is no buying, selling or trading of shares on the platform, and no promise of profits or returns. Agreement terms are shown before you subscribe.
