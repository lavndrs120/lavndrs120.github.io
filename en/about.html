About Lavenders (LAVNDR120)

Last updated: 1 November 2026

What is Lavenders?

Lavenders (Arabic: لاڤندرز) is a digital market system that brings together 120 businesses and brands in a single market. Each business runs its own order-management app (OMS), and their real orders feed one live consumer index called LAVNDR120, updated moment by moment. Every business in the market can see the performance of the other 119.

The name is a brand for a market system and has no connection to lavender plants or lavender oil.

How the index moves

  1. 120 businesses: each has its own order app.
  2. Every new order raises its business's weight inside the index.
  3. More orders mean a higher index value.

What the home page shows

The home page shows the real “Net Asset Value” card to every visitor: the current value, the daily change, the day's high and low, and volatility. Other screens are demo data for visitors and real data for subscribers.

Terms

  • Lavenders — the system and brand name.
  • LAVNDR120 — its live consumer index.
  • OMS — the order-management system for businesses and brands, on web and Android.
  • Lava — the market data itself, read-only.
  • Vindex — the Oracle forecasting report, limited to 120 members.
  • Magma — a limited (500-member) membership to explore the market.
