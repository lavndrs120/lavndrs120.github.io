Lava: read-only market data

Last updated: 1 November 2026

What is Lava?

Lavenders started with 120 businesses and brands; when their orders came together, a market appeared with movement, a consumer index and data that grows with every move. That data is called Lava.

Lava is the market data itself, read-only. The 120 businesses form the market, and you read the data without having to be one of them.

What is included

  • The monthly archive.
  • The most-requested products and services in the market.

Who it is for

Anyone who wants to follow the market without joining the 120. Lava belongs to the Join Up path.

What it does not include

Lava is read-only. There is no buying, selling or trading of shares on the platform, and no promise of profits or returns. Agreement terms are shown before you subscribe.
