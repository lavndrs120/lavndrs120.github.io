OMS: order management for your business or brand

Last updated: 1 November 2026

What is OMS?

OMS stands for Order Management System. It runs on web and Android and gives each business or brand its own order system, so it stays close to the phones of the people who use its service or product.

How it relates to the index

Every order a business records in OMS raises its weight inside the LAVNDR120 index, so your order system becomes part of a market of 120 businesses, and each business sees the performance of the other 119.

OMS vs Lava

  • OMS is the system a business uses to manage its orders.
  • Lava is the market data, read-only; you do not need to be one of the 120.

Who it is for

Service and product businesses and brands. OMS belongs to the Economic Movement path. See For businesses.
