Lavenders reveals consumer behaviour from the real orders of 120 businesses

Press release · Global · 17 November 2026

Lavenders, a digital market system, has made its live consumer index, LAVNDR120, publicly visible. The index is built from the real orders of 120 businesses and brands, showing what people actually order, when, and from which businesses.

Each business in the market runs its own order-management app (OMS) on web and Android. Their combined order activity feeds LAVNDR120, and reports built on the same data are available to businesses and brands.

Two tools for businesses

App builder. A business can create a web store and an Android app inside its own Google account. Customers order with an email one-time code, and the owner accepts or rejects each order. Read more.

Golden certificate. An optional certificate available to businesses with at least 100 documented orders, subject to review by the administration. Read more.
